05 / RIOT INTEGRATION

WHY
VALORANT DATA?

Official game data can provide the match and round context that the current prototype has to detect locally.

With an approved Riot integration, Roundly is intended to use permitted information such as match identity, map and mode, agent, round state, score, result and relevant performance context where available and appropriate to the product.

The purpose is contextualization and automation: helping Roundly associate the player's own observations with the correct match and round, rather than generating real-time tactical advice.

Player consent: Riot data access will follow the applicable Riot requirements, including the required player opt-in flow for accessing player data.

08 / PRODUCT SCOPE

BUILT FOR
SELF-REVIEW.

Roundly is focused on player-generated observations, personal match context and post-match VOD review.

It is not designed as an opponent scouting tool, a real-time tactical recommendation system, or a mechanism for telling players what action to take during a match.

Any future Riot integration will be implemented according to the applicable Riot Developer Policy and product requirements.
