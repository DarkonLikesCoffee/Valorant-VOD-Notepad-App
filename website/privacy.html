How the current Roundly application handles information.

LAST UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 2026

01 / SCOPE

This Privacy Policy describes the information handled by the current version of Roundly, an independent fan-made VALORANT VOD review application.

This policy describes the application as it exists today. Features that are not currently implemented, including Riot Sign On (RSO) and Riot Games API integrations, are not described as current data-processing activities.

02 / INFORMATION HANDLED

Information created by you

Roundly can handle content you create while using the application, including VOD review notes, scratch-pad content, session information, and generated review documents.

Application settings

Roundly stores the settings and preferences required for the application to operate according to your configuration.

Technical information

The application may process technical information necessary for its operation, such as local application state, files and operating-system information required by its features.

Roundly does not currently require a user account, collect a Riot password, operate analytics, or operate a Roundly-hosted backend for storing your reviews.

03 / LOCAL STORAGE

The current standalone application is designed around local storage. Review content, session history, scratch-pad content and application settings are stored on the user's device rather than in a Roundly online account.

If you remove local application data or delete a stored session through the application, that local data may no longer be available to Roundly or to the application.

04 / SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES

Roundly does not currently sell your personal information or operate advertising or analytics services.

The current application does not send your review notes or locally stored review documents to a Roundly-operated server.

Third-party services may be involved in the normal operation or distribution of the software in ways outside Roundly's local storage, but Roundly does not currently operate a separate online data-processing service for your reviews.

05 / YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights regarding personal information, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability.

Because the current review data is stored locally, many requests concerning review content can be handled directly by you through the files and controls available on your device.

Future Riot integration: If Roundly later introduces RSO or Riot Games API functionality, this policy will be reviewed and updated before those features are made available where required. The updated policy will describe the data accessed, the purposes of processing, retention and any applicable sharing.

06 / CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Roundly's functionality, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest version published on this website will apply to the current website and application practices described in it.
